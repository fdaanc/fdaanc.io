--- title: "FDAANC Board Election for 2014-2015" date: 2013-10-14 ---

Dear members,

It’s time for the board election of year 2014 to 2015.  If you would like to serve on the board, please complete the following online form by October 20, 2013.

The only requirements for board members are:

  • Be physically present in the Northern California area for at least 50% of the time in a   single year
  • Participate in and be a volunteer at least 50% of the events in a single year
