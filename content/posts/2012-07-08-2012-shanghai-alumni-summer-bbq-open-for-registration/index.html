2012 SHANGHAI ALUMNI SUMMER BBQ

浦江之夏 上海高校校友夏季烧烤
Aug 26, 2012  Mountain View

2012 “Summer of Pu Jiang” BBQ will be held in August. Please mark your calendar to join this biggest event of the year, co-hosted by six Shanghai alumni associations: FDAANC, SJTU-SV, ECNUAA, ECUSTAA, SUTCMAA and TJUAASFB. Summer BBQ has become a popular tradition among our alumni and friends, with a history over a decade.

Over 300 people attend this event each year at Rengstorff Park in Mountain View, centered in Silicon Valley.  It is a great opportunity to enjoy the summer, relax your body and mind, catch up with old friends, make new ones, and to have fun!

Highlights:
  • Delicious food including beef, chicken, Korea BBQ and fruits.
  • Yu Lan Cup Volleyball Tournament with teams is from SJTU-SV, FDAANC and other fellow alumni associations will be opened at the events.
  • The Pu Jiang Cup Tug-Of-War game is the best exacting at the BBQ.
  • Pinyada, water balloon tired race and water melon eating race for both kids and adults.
